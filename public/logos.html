Thirty-six marks for Undefiled Religion. The first sixteen come from something real in the project: the ledger, the verse, the maize, the two of you. Numbers 17 to 26 are quieter and come from scripture about giving. Numbers 27 to 36 each draw one named verse, listed with it. Toggle the background and the size to judge them properly, then tell me the numbers you like and I will take those further.

Every mark is a single flat colour so it works stamped on a receipt, a shirt, or a favicon. Nothing here uses a photograph or a person. There is no cross either, even among the scripture ones: the project is not a church and the mark should not say it is. Bread, oil, a lamp, a cup, a seed and a field carry the same meaning without claiming an institution.
